## Executive Summary

Every person deserves a way to prove who they are — simply, privately, and with dignity.

ChainID is a sovereign digital identity system designed from the ground up for real humans in the real world. It began in Wenatchee, Washington, when local businessman and former mayoral candidate James McLaughlin asked a simple question: *How do we help people experiencing homelessness register for basic services when they have lost every piece of paper that proves they exist?*

That single human-centered question became the foundation of ChainID.

The solution is elegant and intuitive: a physical kiosk that feels like an Apple product — calm, premium, and effortless — paired with the Avalanche Core wallet. In under 60 seconds, anyone can create a cryptographically verifiable identity that lives safely on the blockchain and in their pocket. No seed phrases to memorize. No fragile paper documents. Just a secure, private proof they control completely.

## The Problem

Physical IDs get lost, stolen, or destroyed by the elements.  
Centralized databases get breached.  
People fall through the cracks — especially the unhoused, migrants, students, patients, and anyone who simply needs to prove who they are without handing over their entire life story.

## The ChainID Solution

ChainID turns identity into something you **own**, not something you borrow.

Using hardware-secured kiosks and the Avalanche Core mobile wallet, ChainID creates a privacy-first, zero-knowledge digital credential. Your raw personal data is never stored. Only mathematical proofs live on the blockchain and IPFS. The result feels magical: you walk up, verify once, and forever carry an immutable, shareable proof of identity you control.

## How It Works – The User Experience

Designed with Apple-level clarity and common sense:

1. **Approach the Kiosk**  
   The screen wakes gently as you step near. No buttons. No menus. A calm interface greets you.

2. **Connect Your Phone**  
   Scan one QR code with Avalanche Core. Your existing wallet links instantly (secured by Apple/Google login — no seed phrase needed).

3. **Verify Privately**  
   Place your ID on the scanner and look at the camera. Enter sensitive information (such as SSN) on your own phone — never on the public kiosk. Passive liveness confirms you are real.

4. **Instant, Secure Mint**  
   Your data is hashed inside a hardware secure enclave (TPM 2.0), permanently wiped from memory, and minted as a gasless NFT directly into your Core wallet via ERC-4337.

You walk away with a beautiful, scannable digital credential you fully control.

## Core Technical Principles

- **Common Sense First** — Every step is obvious and intuitive.  
- **Privacy by Design** — Zero-knowledge hashes only. Raw data is never stored.  
- **Hardware Security** — TPM 2.0 + full memory zeroization (Rust `zeroize`).  
- **Sovereignty** — You own your identity. No company or government can revoke it.  
- **Future-Proof** — Permanent storage on IPFS (InterPlanetary File System).  
- **Gasless & Frictionless** — ERC-4337 Account Abstraction (the kiosk pays the gas).  
- **Native Avalanche Core Integration** — Works seamlessly with seed abstraction.
